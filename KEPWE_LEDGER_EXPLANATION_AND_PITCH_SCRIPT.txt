2. KEPWE LEDGER KYA HAI? (Executive Overview)
================================================================================

KEPWE Ledger sirf ek simple invoice maker ya cashbook nahi hai. Yeh ek complete 
"Enterprise-Grade Double-Entry General Ledger aur Indian Tax Engine" hai.

Iske 4 Fundamental Rules hain:
1. MATHEMATICAL INVARIANT:
   - Sum(Debits) === Sum(Credits) (har transaction me delta = 0.00).
   - Assets === Liabilities + Equity (Balance Sheet hamesha match rahegi).
2. TRANSACTION IMMUTABILITY:
   - Posted journals ko kabhi database se delete ya dirty edit nahi kiya ja sakta.
   - Agar kisi transaction me galti hui, toh system offsetting Reversal Entry (REV-...) generate karta hai audit compliance ke liye.
3. ZERO IN-MEMORY DATA LOSS:
   - Koi bhi temporary memory array ya mock storage use nahi hota.
   - PGlite embedded PostgreSQL persistent storage (`backend/data/pgdata`) par har line immediately commit hoti hai. Process restart hone par bhi 100% data intact rehta hai.
4. INDIAN STATUTORY COMPLIANCE COMPLIANT:
   - GST: CGST, SGST, IGST, Place of Supply (POS), HSN/SAC codes, Rule 88A set-off.
   - TDS: Sections 194C, 194J, 194I, 194H, 206AA (Higher 20% on Missing PAN), Challan ITNS 281 with 7-digit BSR code.
   - Payroll: EPF (12%), ESIC (0.75% / 3.25%), Professional Tax state slabs.
   - Fixed Assets: SLM (Straight Line) & WDV (Written Down Value) depreciation schedules.

3. CORE ARCHITECTURE — 15 PILLARS KA DETAIL EXPLANATION
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 1: Indian Standard Chart of Accounts (COA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Har company ke register hote hi 43 standard accounts automatically create hote hain:
  - 1000s = ASSETS: 1010 Bank Current A/c, 1030 Accounts Receivable (Sundry Debtors), 1050 Input CGST (ITC), 1051 Input SGST (ITC), 1061 Fixed Assets (Computers/IT), 1090 Accumulated Depreciation.
  - 2000s = LIABILITIES: 2010 Accounts Payable (Sundry Creditors), 2020 Output CGST Payable, 2021 Output SGST Payable, 2030 TDS Payable (194J/194C), 2040 EPF Payable, 2050 Salaries Payable.
  - 3000s = EQUITY: 3010 Owner / Shareholder Capital, 3020 Retained Earnings.
  - 4000s = REVENUE: 4010 Sales Revenue (Goods), 4020 Service Revenue.
  - 5000s = EXPENSES: 5010 COGS, 5020 Salaries & Wages, 5040 Legal & Professional Fees, 5080 Depreciation Expense.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 2: Double-Entry Journal Engine (Zero-Tolerance Validation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- System kisi bhi aise journal ko accept nahi karega jo balanced na ho.
- Agar Debit ₹10,00,000 hai aur Credit ₹9,99,999 hai, system immediately 'UnbalancedJournalError' throw karega.
- Negative amounts aur dual debit/credit lines strict reject hote hain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 3: GST Invoicing Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Place of Supply (POS) ke basis par automatically decide karta hai:
  - Intra-State (same state): 9% CGST + 9% SGST.
  - Inter-State (different state): 18% IGST.
- Invoice generate hote hi automatically linked balanced double-entry journal post hota hai:
  - Debit: 1030 Accounts Receivable (Gross Total)
  - Credit: 4010 Sales Revenue (Taxable Subtotal)
  - Credit: 2020 Output CGST (Tax amount)
  - Credit: 2021 Output SGST (Tax amount)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 4: Accounts Receivable (AR) & Customer Collections
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Partial payments aur full payments track karta hai.
- Overpayment Protection: Agar invoice ₹2,95,000 ka hai aur customer ₹3,00,000 pay karne ki koshish kare, system excess payment ko strictly reject kar deta hai.
- Payment receive hote hi: Debit 1010 Bank, Credit 1030 Accounts Receivable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 5: Accounts Payable (AP) & Vendor Bills with Section 194J TDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Jab vendor bill enter hota hai (e.g. Legal Fees ₹1,00,000 + 18% GST ₹18,000):
  - System Section 194J ke mutabiq 10% TDS (₹10,000) withhold karta hai.
  - Net Payable to Vendor banta hai: ₹1,08,000.
  - Journal entry auto-post hoti hai:
      Dr 5040 Legal & Professional Fees = ₹1,00,000 (Expense)
      Dr 1050 Input CGST (Eligible ITC) = ₹9,000
      Dr 1051 Input SGST (Eligible ITC) = ₹9,000
      Cr 2030 TDS Payable (Sec 194J)   = ₹10,000 (Govt liability)
      Cr 2010 Accounts Payable          = ₹1,08,000 (Vendor net liability)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 6: Vendor Disbursement via Bank
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Vendor ko NEFT/RTGS se payment karne par Accounts Payable clear ho jata hai:
  - Debit 2010 Accounts Payable ₹1,08,000
  - Credit 1010 Bank Account ₹1,08,000

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 7: Bank Statement Import & Multi-Tier Automated Reconciliation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bank statement (CSV/JSON/API) ingest hota hai.
- Multi-tier matching algorithm:
  1. Exact Match: Transaction Date + Ref Number + Exact Amount match hone par internal books se link kar deta hai.
  2. Auto Match (±2 days window): Cheque/clearing delay ko accommodate karta hai.
  3. Unmatched Queue: Jo transaction books me nahi the (jaise Bank SMS charge ya interest), unko CA review queue me suggest karta hai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 8: Indian Statutory Payroll Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Employee salary structures automatically calculate karta hai:
  - Basic + HRA + Special Allowance = Gross Salary.
  - Employee Provident Fund (EPF): 12% deduction on Basic/Gross ceiling.
  - Professional Tax (PT): State slab ke hisab se (e.g., Maharashtra ₹200/month).
  - Net Salary = Gross - EPF - PT.
- Salary Run hote hi balanced journal post hota hai:
  - Dr 5020 Salaries Expense (Gross)
  - Cr 2040 EPF Payable
  - Cr 2042 PT Payable
  - Cr 2050 Salaries Payable (Net)
- Jab salaries bank se disburse hoti hain: Dr 2050 Salaries Payable, Cr 1010 Bank.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 9: Fixed Capital Assets & Depreciation Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Asset Register: Asset Name, Purchase Cost, Useful Life (years), Salvage Value.
- Methods: Straight Line Method (SLM) & Written Down Value (WDV).
- Automated Depreciation Run:
  - Dr 5080 Depreciation Expense (P&L expense)
  - Cr 1090 Accumulated Depreciation (Contra-Asset on Balance Sheet)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 10: Financial Report 1 — Trial Balance
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sabhi 43 ledgers ki debit/credit lines aggregate karta hai.
- Proves that Total Debits === Total Credits. Delta hamesha ₹0.00 hota hai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 11: Financial Report 2 — Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Operating Revenue vs Operating Expenses (COGS, Salaries, Professional Fees, Depreciation).
- Gross Profit, Operating Profit, aur Net Profit compute karta hai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 12: Financial Report 3 — Balance Sheet
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Fundamental Accounting Equation:
    TOTAL ASSETS === TOTAL LIABILITIES + SHAREHOLDER EQUITY
- Equation difference = ₹0.00.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 13: Indian GST Engine — GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B (Rule 88A Set-off)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- GSTR-1: Outward supplies summary, B2B invoices, HSN Summary Table 12.
- GSTR-3B: Rule 88A ke mutabiq statutory set-off order:
  1. IGST Credit sabse pehle IGST liability, phir CGST/SGST liability me exhaust hoga.
  2. CGST Credit sirf CGST aur IGST ke liye use ho sakta hai (SGST ke liye kabhi nahi).
  3. SGST Credit sirf SGST aur IGST ke liye use ho sakta hai (CGST ke liye kabhi nahi).
  4. Baki bacha amount Net Cash Payable (Challan PMT-06) generate hota hai.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 14: TDS Form 26Q & Challan ITNS 281
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Quarterly TDS Return (Form 26Q) generate karta hai with deductee PAN and withholding details.
- Central Government deposit ke liye Challan ITNS 281 record karta hai with 7-digit BSR Code, CIN, aur assessment year.
- Journal: Dr 2030 TDS Payable, Cr 1010 Bank.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PILLAR 15: Compliance Engine & 59 Statutory Obligations Calendar
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Entity type (Pvt Ltd, LLP, Prop) ke hisab se 59 statutory deadlines automatically generate karta hai:
  - GST: 11th (GSTR-1), 20th (GSTR-3B).
  - TDS: 7th of every month (Challan 281), Quarterly 31st (Form 26Q).
  - PF/ESIC: 15th of every month (ECR filing).
  - Advance Tax: 15 June (15%), 15 Sept (45%), 15 Dec (75%), 15 March (100%).
  - MCA: AOC-4 (Financials within 30 days of AGM), MGT-7 (Annual Return).
- Multi-Stage CA Approval Gate:
    DRAFT  -->  SYSTEM VALIDATED  -->  APPROVED BY CA  -->  PROVIDER SUBMISSION GATE

7. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (CA & Technical Auditors ke Questions)
================================================================================

Q1: "Kya yeh Tally ya QuickBooks se better hai?"
Ans: Haan, kyunki Tally desktop-based hai aur usme automatic statutory tax offsets 
     (jaise Rule 88A) aur multi-party real-time workflow nahi hota. KEPWE Ledger me 
     invoicing, banking, payroll, aur tax returns sab ek hi mathematical double-entry 
     database se interconnected hain.

Q2: "Agar user kisi entry me galti kar de, toh kya wo edit kar sakta hai?"
Ans: Nahi. Accounting standard ke mutabiq posted journals immutable hote hain. 
     System automatically ek offsetting Reversal Entry (REV-...) banata hai, 
     jisse audit trail 100% clean aur statutory compliant rehti hai.

Q3: "TDS section 194J aur 194C me PAN na hone par kya hota hai?"
Ans: KEPWE Ledger me Section 206AA rule enforce kiya gaya hai. Agar vendor ka PAN 
     missing ya invalid hai, toh system automatically higher rate (20%) TDS deduct 
     karta hai.

Q4: "Server restart hone par kya data udd jayega?"
Ans: Bilkul nahi. Data persistent PostgreSQL me stored hai (backend/data/pgdata). 
     Zero in-memory storage policy hai. System reboot hone par bhi 100% data intact rehta hai.
